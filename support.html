Common questions

How does the try-on work?
Add a portrait in the studio, pick a style, choose the length, braid size and colour, and tap Create my look. After a minute or so you'll see a before-and-after slider and a short style note.

What photo works best?
A clear, front-facing portrait in natural light, with your face and hairline visible, no filters, and only you in the picture. Leave a little space around your head.

Why doesn't my preview look exactly like the example?
Previews are AI-generated inspiration, not a guaranteed salon result. Your final style depends on your own hair, your braider's technique and your extensions. If a detail is off, try a clearer photo or another length or braid size.

How many looks can I create?
Your first look is included. Braid Muse Plus unlocks up to 20 previews a day.

How do I restore a subscription on a new phone?
Tap Plus on the Discover tab, then Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us: manage your subscriptions.

Where are my photos stored?
Saved looks stay on your iPhone. Your photo is sent for processing only when you create a look, and our server doesn't keep it. See the privacy policy.

How do I delete a look?
Open it in My looks and delete it. Deleting the app removes all saved looks.
