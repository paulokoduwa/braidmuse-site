Privacy Policy

Braid Muse · last updated 25 September 2026

Braid Muse lets you preview braid styles on your own photo. It has no account, no login and no advertising, and it does not track you across other apps or websites. This policy describes every piece of data the app handles.

Your photo

Your photo leaves your iPhone only when you tap Create my look or ask for a style note, and only after you have agreed to send it. The app first shrinks it to a small JPEG, then sends it with the style choices you picked (style, length, braid size and colour) to our server.

  • Our server passes the photo to Google's Gemini API, which creates the preview image and the style note and sends them back.
  • Our server does not save your photo, the preview or the style note. It forwards them and forgets them.
  • Google processes the photo to produce the result under the Gemini API terms, which allow it to keep request data for a limited time to detect abuse.

Please use only a photo of yourself, or one you have permission to use.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Looks you save, with their original photos, previews and style notes
  • Styles you add to your wishlist, and your style vibe
  • Whether you have already used your included look

This is stored in the app's own storage on your device. We cannot see it. Deleting a look in My looks removes it, and deleting the app removes everything.

Usage limits

To keep the service fair, our server counts how many previews and style notes are created each day. The count is stored against a random identifier created by the app, not your name, email or Apple Account. On a real iPhone the app also uses Apple's DeviceCheck service, which lets Apple remember a single yes-or-no answer for your device: whether its included look has been used. We never receive a device identifier from it.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details. To know what you have paid for, Braid Muse uses RevenueCat, which receives the same random identifier and your purchase history. Our server asks RevenueCat whether that identifier has an active subscription before creating a preview.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data for advertising.
  • We don't identify you from your photo, and the AI is instructed not to guess your identity, ethnicity, health or personality.
  • We don't use your photos to train AI models.

Children

Braid Muse is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
